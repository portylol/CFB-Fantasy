How PPE scoring works

You can always predict a winner. Score prediction is optional. Each team's guessed score lands in exactly one bucket — exact, within 3, or within 7 — never more than one, so a bucket bonus is never double-counted for the same team.

Correct winner+10
1 team's score within 7 (not closer)+5
Both teams' scores within 7 (not closer)+10
1 team's score within 3+8
Both teams' scores within 3+15
Winning team's exact score+10
Losing team's exact score+5
Exact final score (both teams)+25
Quarter score exact (1 team, chosen quarter)+5
Quarter score exact (both teams, chosen quarter)+10

Winner/loser/exact-score bonuses are separate checks from the within-3/within-7 buckets and can stack with them. Quarter betting compares the cumulative score at the end of whichever quarter(s) you fill in and needs quarter-by-quarter data from a live source. Results are calculated from completed games returned by the stats API.
